PandaCode 0.2.12 fixes OpenCode model selection.

- Fixes valid models being rejected with “The harness does not advertise the selected setting” when OpenCode reports a large model catalog. Models later in the list, including DeepSeek Flash, can now start normally.
- Keeps model discovery and launch validation on the same bounded catalog size, including grouped model lists. Unsupported models and reasoning efforts are still checked against the harness's current options.

Your saved models and task settings are preserved. This update is for Apple silicon Macs.
