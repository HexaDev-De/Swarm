Team setup now opens as a compact popover beside the composer. Choose a saved profile, architect model, and worker models without opening a full modal. Run limits stay tucked away until needed.

Architects and workers can exchange direct messages across harnesses. Open Workers → Team messages to see questions and handoffs. Workers can also save progress checkpoints with completed work, next steps, and blockers; the latest checkpoint appears in each worker’s details and is restored when interrupted work resumes.

New messages and blockers can wake an idle architect within the configured round budget. Coordination is event-driven, with bounded messages and summaries. Existing concurrency, file-scope scheduling, approval, cancellation, and retry limits remain in place.

Saved teams and profiles are retained. Existing Codex teams transfer their saved conversation once on the next explicit run to enable the new tools. Updating never starts an agent automatically.
