# Attachments, mockups, and clearer project navigation

- Attach images, documents, and folders from the message box, with image paste and drag-and-drop support.
- Create and edit mockup sketches from the attachment menu and send them to your coding agent.
- Mention folders with `@` alongside project files.
- Make hidden tasks easier to find when the sidebar is filtered to one project.
- Keep the Team button's appearance consistent before and after opening its selector.

PDF and Office document text extraction is available on macOS. Images, plain-text documents, folders, and sketches are supported across the desktop platforms.
