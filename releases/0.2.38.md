# PandaCode 0.2.38

- **Bots in the sidebar.** A Bots section at the bottom of the sidebar lists your bots and shows when one is working or paused. Click a bot to chat with it right in the main conversation view, with the full composer. Use + to add a bot, and the gear to open its memory, schedules, channels and rules. Bot workspaces no longer appear among your projects.
- **Slimmer modals.** Fields, dropdowns, buttons, setting rows and tabs in every modal are more compact, with a smaller title and close button.

Known limitation: messages with images or moodboards sent in a bot's chat go to the task directly, without refreshing the bot's memories.

This is an Alpha preview. macOS uses ad-hoc signing without Apple notarization;
Windows does not yet have Authenticode signing. All platform updates retain
cryptographic signature verification. Linux requires X11/XWayland.
