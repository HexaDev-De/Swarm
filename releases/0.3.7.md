# Agent team threads read in order

## Agent teams

- **Worker lines stay where they were spawned.** While the architect's turn runs, its worker lines appear right after the call that started them. Everything the architect does after that, including its tool calls once the workers finish, now appears below them instead of above.
- **A resumed architect turn stays below earlier workers.** When the architect picks up again after its workers finish, the earlier worker lines keep their place above the new turn.

## Known limitations

- If the architect starts workers twice in one turn, the second batch appears with the first.
