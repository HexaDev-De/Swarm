# PandaCode 0.2.13

- Watch Jev test your app directly in the sidebar browser, with a live view of its isolated Chrome session and a compact action history.
- Open Test with Jev in a small browser popover. Choose a goal or preset, then start watching without leaving your task.
- Stop a test, return to normal browsing while it continues, or reopen a saved test from its report.
- Keep live previews private and lightweight: bounded local frames, capture only while watched, and automatic cleanup when the viewer closes or disconnects.

Jev remains an optional add-on. Browser testing requires its runtime and your provider keys; watching a test adds no model calls.

This is an Apple silicon Alpha preview. macOS uses ad-hoc signing while Apple Developer enrollment is pending; updates remain cryptographically signed and verified.
