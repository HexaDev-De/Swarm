# A shorter OpenCode picker and a faster iPhone Duo

## Harnesses

- **OpenCode's picker stays short.** With no models chosen in Settings → Agents → OpenCode, the model picker shows Default and five models: the one the task uses, then the ones you used most recently. Type to search every OpenCode model; a model you pick from search moves to the top next time. A selection saved in Settings works as before.

## Simulators

- **iPhone Duo looks like the real thing from behind.** The back now has the wide camera plateau with two lenses, the mic and flash, the Apple logo, a matte titanium finish and the straight hinge edge.
- **Folding is instant.** Closed, Book and Open move the 3D Duo the moment you click. The display shows a spinner until its screen arrives, and a fold that fails moves back and shows the error.
- **Faster screens.** A fold reaches the new display in about half a second instead of about 1.5 seconds, and the inner display streams compressed frames, about three times faster and twelve times smaller than before.

## Known limitations

- iPhone Duo needs Xcode 27.1 or later on macOS.
- Timings were measured on one Mac with a booted Duo.
- Browser tests that already failed in 0.3.15 still fail; they are unrelated to these changes.
