# Faster, snappier, and approvals right above the prompt

## Faster agents, fewer tokens

- **Follow-ups start about twice as fast with Claude Code.** After a turn, the next turn's process starts in the background, so your follow-up skips the CLI's startup (about 4 s to the first word instead of 8–9 s in our tests). At most two wait at a time, each for up to 10 minutes.
- **About 650 fewer tokens on every message.** PandaCode's fixed instructions (sidebar titles, the run script, change tracking) now go to Claude Code, GLM and MiMo once as a cached system prompt instead of being added to each message. Other harnesses get them on the first message and every eighth.

## An instant interface

- **No transitions.** Menus, dialogs, panels and settings appear at once. Loading spinners still run.
- **Switching threads is one frame.** A thread you return to appears formatted and scrolled to the bottom, with no loading flash or jump.
- **The prompt stays put.** It sits in the same place in every thread.
- **⌘/Ctrl + and −** now scale the whole interface, icons included, not just text.

## Approvals and teams

- **A slim approval bar above the prompt.** Each request is one line with **Deny** and **Approve**; several group into one line with **Approve all**. Long commands no longer overflow.
- **Spawned workers are one line each**, in the turn that started them. Click one to open it in the Architect & workers panel, which is now solid, compact and has no border.

## Find, GitHub and Git

- **Find in thread.** Press **⌘/Ctrl F** to search your messages and the agent's, then Enter or ⌘G to step through matches.
- **GitHub threads open beside the GitHub view**, like the board's side panel, so you can keep chatting without leaving it.
- **Merge into main.** A worktree task's branch menu merges its committed work into your main branch locally, then offers to remove the worktree. Uncommitted changes and conflicts are refused without changing anything.
- **Switch branches** from the branch chip, including straight back to `main`.
- **Merge a linked pull request** from the task's PR tab.
- In the GitHub view, repositories without a push in 30 days fold under **Other**.

## Look and feel

- **Settings is a full page** with grouped sections. Resource usage moved there from the status bar, which is gone.
- **Sidebar:**
  - Threads in **All projects** are one line under their project's icon.
  - With one project chosen, they're two lines, and the project's icon leads the filter.
  - The selected thread is a solid fill of your accent color.
- **A Bots view** in the sidebar lists each bot with its latest message.
- **Solid, borderless surfaces:** dropdowns, modals and the prompt box drop their glass and borders. Corners are tighter throughout.
- **Bot conversations** no longer show the bot's brief as "Attached context".

## Fixes

- The bot row's settings gear no longer slips out from under the pointer when pressed.
- The Team status line stays inside the conversation column.
- The moodboard list is one tidy line per board.
